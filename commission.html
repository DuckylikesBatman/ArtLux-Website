    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does a commission typically take?

    Most commissions take 2–3 weeks from concept approval to completion. Larger or more complex works may take a little longer. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

    What materials do you use?

    We use only archival-quality materials to ensure your artwork stands the test of time:

    • Professional-grade acrylics and oils
    • Gallery-wrapped canvases and premium panels
    • Acid-free papers and mounting boards
    • UV-protective varnishes for longevity
    Do you offer international shipping?

    Yes, we offer worldwide shipping with professional custom crating, full insurance coverage, and tracked delivery to ensure your artwork arrives safely.

    Can I see progress updates?

    Absolutely! We provide regular progress photos and updates throughout the creation process. We want you to be involved and excited as your vision comes to life.

    How does on-site work like murals or ceiling painting work?

    For murals, arabesque, and other wall or ceiling work, we begin with a site visit to assess the space. Floors, furniture, and fixtures are fully protected before any painting begins, and the number of days on site depends on the scale of the piece — we'll walk you through this during your consultation. Payment is arranged in stages tied to project milestones.
