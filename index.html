What We Do

Three crafts, one studio — from the first brushstroke on a wall to the final coat on a cherished piece of furniture.

  1. Decorative Wall & Ceiling Painting

    Luxury finishes, hand-painted murals, gold leaf, and arabesque patterns for refined interiors.

  2. Furniture Painting & Restoration

    Expert refinishing, custom-painted finishes, and antique restoration to revive furniture.

  3. Bespoke Artistic Paintings

    Custom canvas art and statement pieces designed to complement any space.
